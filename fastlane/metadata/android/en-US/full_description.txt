<i>Xtra Lite</i> is a lightweight Twitch player and browser, based on <a href="https://github.com/crackededed/Xtra">Xtra</a>. It keeps the player, browsing and downloads, and removes chat.

<b>Differences from Xtra</b>

* <b>No chat.</b> Live chat, chat replay, chat downloads, emotes and chat settings are removed. Login only requests the user:read:follows scope.
* <b>Skip silence</b> for videos, clips and downloads, as in Twire. Toggle it from the player menu.
* <b>Swipe for brightness and volume</b> in fullscreen: left third for brightness, right third for volume. Can be turned off in Settings → Player.
* <b>Channel panel</b> below the video in portrait, with the streamer's profile and recent videos.
* <b>Lower data use:</b> audio-only playback in the background, 480p default and a 25 s buffer on mobile data, smaller thumbnails, tabs that load only when opened.
* <b>Cast to TV</b> with Google Cast (needs Google Play services and the same Wi-Fi as the TV). Media controls in the notification, and progress of past broadcasts is saved for resuming on the phone.
* <b>5 app icons</b> under Settings → Theme → App icon.

<b>Kept from Xtra</b>

* Watch live streams, past broadcasts and clips
* Download videos and clips to watch offline
* Picture-in-picture and background audio
* Sleep timer
* Follow channels and games without logging in

Xtra Lite installs as a separate app and can be installed next to Xtra.
